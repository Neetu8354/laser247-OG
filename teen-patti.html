How to Play Teen Patti

Teen Patti is a 3-card poker game played with a standard 52-card deck. The objective is to have the best 3-card hand and maximize the pot. Hand rankings from highest to lowest: Trail (Three of a Kind), Pure Sequence (Straight Flush), Sequence (Straight), Color (Flush), Pair, and High Card.
